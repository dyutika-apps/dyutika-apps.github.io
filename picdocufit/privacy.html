Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the PicDocuFit app (the "App"), made by Dyutika ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy and to our Terms of Use.

Summary

  • Your photos, signatures and documents are processed only on your phone. We never upload them and never see them.
  • We have no accounts, no sign-up and no servers that collect your personal information.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information as described below. Buying "Remove ads" stops ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • Face finding and background change use Google ML Kit on your phone. Your photos are not sent anywhere; ML Kit only sends Google anonymous technical statistics (see section 4).

1. Photos, signatures and documents

When you pick or take a photo, or open a PDF, the App reads it on your device to resize, crop or compress it. The result is saved where you choose (Gallery, a folder, or the App's "My Files" list) and shared only when you tap Share. These files never leave your phone because of the App. You can delete them in "My Files" or by uninstalling the App.

Any name or date you type for a photo stamp is used only to draw that text on your picture and is not stored or sent anywhere.

2. Permissions

  • Camera — only when you choose to take a photo inside the App.
  • Photos / files — only the photos or PDFs you pick, and to save results to your Gallery (older Android versions ask for storage permission for this).
  • Internet — to show ads, to process purchases, and to fetch updated size presets.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, and app set ID;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interactions (views, clicks) and basic device and app information;
  • diagnostic and performance data about ads.

This data is collected and used by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive information that identifies you personally.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the App asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown. You can change it any time from the App menu › Privacy settings (shown where applicable). On any Android phone you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

4. On-device AI (Google ML Kit)

"Auto-fit face" and "background change" use Google ML Kit, which runs entirely on your phone: your photos are never sent to Google or to us. To measure and improve its features, ML Kit sends Google technical, non-personal information such as device model and Android version, app name and version, the speed of the feature and error codes. This is encrypted in transit and not passed to third parties. See ML Kit terms and ML Kit data disclosure.

5. Purchases (Google Play)

"Remove ads" is bought through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment under the Google Play Terms and Google's privacy policy. The App only receives confirmation that the purchase is valid, so it can turn ads off. We never receive your card, UPI or bank details. A note that you own "Remove ads" is stored on your device.

6. Size presets

The App may download an updated list of size presets (a plain file with sizes, no personal data) from the internet. As with any internet request, the server sees your IP address; we do not use it to identify you.

7. What we do not do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your photos or documents for any purpose. We do not build profiles about you. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Data security and retention

Because your files stay on your device, their security depends on your phone's own protection (screen lock, encryption). Results kept in "My Files" stay until you delete them or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google for ads is retained under Google's policies.

9. Children

The App is a general-audience tool for students and adults and is not directed at children under 13. Users under 18 should use it with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK GDPR, or US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data about you ourselves, most requests are handled through Google (see the links above) or by deleting files on your device. You can still contact us at any time.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above will change, and important changes will be shown in the App.
