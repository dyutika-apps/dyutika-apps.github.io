Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Syllabuddy app (the "App"), made by Dyutika ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy and to our Terms of Use.

Summary

  • Your planner data (subjects, timetable, tasks, grades, attendance and settings) stays only on your phone. We never receive it.
  • We have no accounts, no sign-up and no servers that collect your personal information.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information as described below. Buying "Remove ads" stops ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. Data you enter stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored in the App's private storage on your device. When you make a backup, the App creates a file and opens Android's share menu; you choose where that file goes (for example your own Drive or email). Restoring reads a file you pick. Backups are never sent to us. The same applies when you share your timetable with classmates: the file contains only your subjects, classes and due dates (no grades or attendance), and goes only where you send it.

2. Scanning timetables and syllabi

When you choose to scan or upload a photo or PDF, the App reads its text on your device using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. The photo or PDF is not uploaded and the App does not keep it: it is read once, temporary page images are deleted, and only the classes and tasks you confirm are saved in the planner. ML Kit may send Google anonymous information about how the feature performs (for example processing times and errors) to improve it; it does not send your images or the text in them. See Google's ML Kit terms (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • information about ad interactions, app performance and diagnostics.

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. See "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone's Android settings. If you buy "Remove ads", the App stops requesting ads.

4. In-app purchase

The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The App stores only whether ads have been removed.

5. Notifications and the home-screen widget

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. The widget reads data saved by the App on your device. Neither sends data anywhere.

6. Permissions

  • Camera — only when you tap "Take a photo" to scan a timetable or syllabus, through your phone's camera app.
  • Notifications — to show the reminders you ask for.
  • Run at startup — to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Internet / network state — only to load ads and to check purchases with Google Play.

7. Children

The App is intended for students aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the App. Either removes everything the App stored. Because we do not hold any of your planner data, there is nothing else to delete on our side.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. For important changes, the App will ask you to accept the updated documents.
