Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Expiry Radar app (the "App"), made by Dyutika ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy and to our Terms of Use.

Summary

  • Your items, photos and notes are stored only on your phone. We have no servers and never upload them.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-up.
  • Reading the date from a document uses Google ML Kit on your phone. Your photos are not sent anywhere; ML Kit only sends Google anonymous technical statistics (see section 3).
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information as described below. Buying Pro removes ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. What you store in the App

When you add an item you can save a name, category, expiry or renewal date, notes, and an optional photo of the document. All of this stays on your device, in the App's private storage. It is used only to show your list and to schedule your reminders. You can delete any item in the App, or remove everything by uninstalling the App. If you create a backup file, it contains this data and is shared only to the place you choose (for example Google Drive or email).

2. Permissions

  • Camera — only when you choose to scan or photograph a document inside the App.
  • Photos / files — picked through the system photo/document picker; the App does not request storage access and cannot see your other files.
  • Notifications — to show your expiry reminders (Android 13 and newer ask for this).
  • Biometrics / screen lock — only if you turn on App lock, to unlock the App. Handled by Android; we never see your fingerprint or PIN.
  • Internet — to show ads and to process purchases.

3. On-device date reading (Google ML Kit)

Scanning a document and reading its date use Google ML Kit, which runs entirely on your phone: your photos are never sent to Google or to us. To measure and improve its features, ML Kit sends Google technical, non-personal information such as device model and Android version, app name and version, the speed of the feature and error codes. This is encrypted in transit and not passed to third parties. See ML Kit terms and ML Kit data disclosure.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, and app set ID;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interactions (views, clicks) and basic device and app information;
  • diagnostic and performance data about ads.

This data is collected and used by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive information that identifies you personally.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the App asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown. You can change it any time from Settings › Privacy settings (shown where applicable). On any Android phone you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Settings › Google › Ads.

5. Purchases (Google Play)

Pro is bought through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment under the Google Play Terms and Google's privacy policy. The App only receives confirmation that the purchase is valid, so it can unlock Pro. We never receive your card, UPI or bank details. A note that you own Pro is stored on your device.

6. What we do not do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your photos or documents for any purpose of our own. We do not build profiles about you. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Data security and retention

Because your data stays on your device, its security depends on your phone's own protection (screen lock, encryption). Items stay until you delete them or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google for ads is retained under Google's policies.

8. Children

The App is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13. Users under 18 should use it with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK GDPR, or US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data about you ourselves, most requests are handled through Google (see the links above) or by deleting data on your device. You can still contact us at any time.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above will change, and important changes will be shown in the App.
